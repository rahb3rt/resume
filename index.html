How I work

    
Operating principles

    
Patterns that repeat across every practice I've built — from a two-person startup function to a 2,000-engineer organization.

    

      
Paved roads, not mandates
Adoption happens when the right way is the easy way. Reference implementations teams can copy beat standards documents nobody reads.

      
Alert on symptoms, not causes
Page a human only when users are hurting or a budget is burning. Every page that leads to no action gets fixed or deleted.

      
Error budgets over arguments
Reliability as a budgeted, negotiable quantity gives product and engineering a shared currency — the ship-vs-fix debate becomes arithmetic.

      
Prove it with chaos
DR that hasn't been exercised is a theory. Failover paths, PDBs, and multi-region claims get validated continuously, before customers do it for you.

      
Blameless by design
The moment a person gets named as a root cause, the org stops telling itself the truth. Timelines are written from the system's point of view.

      
Automate the toil, keep the people
The engineers who know where the bodies are buried should write the automation. Nobody gets automated out — they get promoted into it.
